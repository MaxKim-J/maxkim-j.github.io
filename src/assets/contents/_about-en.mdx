I have been making a living as a software engineer for several years. I am interested in dealing with complex problems in the realms of life, society, and engineering. I am particularly fascinated by the reactions of individuals or groups when faced with absurd situations. I often think about communities and wish for a world where the minorities do not have to suffer. I majored in English literature.

In this blog, I write about whatever I want to record without making an effort to categorize or organize it. I will write about the software engineering technologies I handle as a professional (tech), the popular media I encounter and consume (culture), and personal essays (essay).
